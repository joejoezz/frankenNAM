Why?

The 12 km NAM ran for over two decades, and forecasters still rely on it.

They reach for it for what it does well: cold-air damming and wedge setups, soundings, precipitation type, and mesoscale structure that coarser models smooth away. NOAA retired the NAM on 3 November 2026.

WOOF-NAM, from Recast Systems, rewrites the NAM for GPUs and runs it daily; its grids are currently free. FrankenNAM shows the other route: the NAM’s own code, which is a US-government work and was public, running on ordinary CPUs on AWS. It costs a few dollars a run, and we explain how to run it yourself. We run it occasionally, not on a schedule: notable events, run on demand and added as cases.
