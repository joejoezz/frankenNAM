What you are buying

Access to the FrankenNAM grid files and their index files while your subscription is active, as described on the Get Operational Data page. The maps on this site are free and have no terms beyond those on this page.

Billing and cancelling

  • Billing is monthly. There is no annual plan and nothing is charged in advance for more than one month.
  • You can cancel at any time in the customer portal that comes with your subscription. Your key keeps working until the end of the month you have paid for, and you are not charged again.
  • Founding members keep the founding price for as long as their subscription stays active. If it lapses, a new subscription is at the price on the Get Operational Data page.

  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details.

The service can stop

This is a small community service run on rented computers. It may be wound down, or changed, for cost, technical or other reasons. If it is wound down we will give a month's notice by email and by a notice on this site, and there will be no further charges after the notice.

Cycles can also be late or missing. The scorecard counts them. Late or missing cycles are not grounds for a refund unless the service is down for most of a billing month. [DECISION NEEDED: confirm this threshold]

Using the data

  • Your key is for you or your organization. Please do not post it publicly or run a service that passes the files on to people who have not subscribed. We may switch off a key that is shared widely.
  • Use is expected to be reasonable: pulling the files you need, not hammering the server. [DECISION NEEDED: any numeric fair-use limit]
  • You may use the data in your own forecasts, products and publications. When you publish a map or number from it, please say it is FrankenNAM and not the operational NAM.

No warranty, and not for safety decisions

FrankenNAM is an experimental model run with no data assimilation. It is provided as is, with no promise that it is accurate, complete or on time. Do not use it as the only basis for decisions about life or property; use National Weather Service forecasts and warnings.

Privacy

We keep your email address, your key and your subscription status, and standard access logs for running and costing the service. We do not sell them. Stripe keeps your payment details under its own terms. [DECISION NEEDED: log retention period]
