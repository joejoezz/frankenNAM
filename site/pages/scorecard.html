How it is scored

  • Surface fields against ASOS stations: 2 m temperature, 2 m dew point, 10 m wind speed, sea-level pressure.
  • Precipitation against MRMS and, for snow, against SNOTEL snow-water and depth changes.
  • Every score is shown for FrankenNAM and for the GFS on the same points, so the comparison is the one a forecaster would make.
  • Scores are by lead time, so the weak first hours (no assimilation) are visible and not averaged away.
