Scorecard

FrankenNAM is the NAM’s physics started from a GFS analysis. It is scored for fidelity to the archived operational NAM and, for context, against observations next to the GFS. It is not offered as a GFS-beater.

No FrankenNAM scores yet. The FrankenNAM column is empty and fills from the hindcast verification runs. The other columns are the baseline the hindcast is judged against.

The archived NAM did not beat the same-cycle GFS against observations on these cases, so the bar for FrankenNAM is closeness to the NAM, not to the GFS. Read the tables with that in mind.

The sample

7 cases, 12-84 h, all against observations: ASOS/AWOS stations, MRMS 24 h precipitation (gauge-corrected) and SNOTEL. 2024111912 PNW bomb cyclone + atmospheric river; 2025010512 Snowcrete, DC (snow to sleet); 2025012100 Gulf Coast snow; 2025031412 Plains severe + blizzard; 2025122912 Lake-effect / Great Lakes snow, late Dec 2025; 2026012312 CAD ice storm, GA/Carolinas; 2026110100 Phase 0 case (autumn, CONUS-wide).

? how to read
Cases were chosen for weather, not at random, and they overlap in time and space, so the pooled numbers are not independent samples of anything. No significance tests are applied. Temperatures use a lapse-rate correction to station height and a 400 m terrain filter, so mountain stations are only partly represented. MRMS under-catches snow in the mountain West. SNOTEL values are coarse (0.1 in per reading) and not terrain-adjusted. Precipitation boxes are 0.25 degrees; the GFS is sampled bilinearly and the NAM by box mean.

Fidelity to the archived NAM

The Phase 1 measure: pooled 12-84 h CONUS RMSE of 2 m temperature, dew point and 10 m wind within 0.1 K, 0.1 K and 0.1 m/s of the archived NAM, and 24 h precipitation ETS within 0.03 at 2.5, 10 and 25 mm.
